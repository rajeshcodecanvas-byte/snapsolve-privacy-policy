Privacy Policy

Application Name: SnapSolve AI: Screen Solver
Package Name: com.rajesh.codecanvas.snapsolveai
Developer / Studio: The CodeCanvas Studio
Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The CodeCanvas Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application, SnapSolve AI: Screen Solver (the "App"). We prioritize user confidentiality, data integrity, and local-first data processing to safeguard your personal privacy.

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

SnapSolve AI is designed to analyze captured screenshots, images, and visual queries to provide instant explanations, translations, and problem resolutions. To enable these functionalities, the App accesses and processes the following data types:

  • Screen Captures & Camera Images: When you trigger a screen capture or use your device camera within the App, visual data (images/screenshots) is processed specifically to extract text and analyze context to execute your requested action (e.g., explain, solve, translate).
  • Solver History Data: The App stores your previous query history, action types, and associated image URIs locally on your device using a local Room database structure. This enables you to review past results quickly within the App's History screen.
  • Device Diagnostics & Performance Data: Basic runtime information (such as crash logs or error states) may be evaluated locally to ensure app stability across different Android OS versions and display densities.

2. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

To deliver advanced query explanations and solver functionality, the visual content captured by you is processed using artificial intelligence services.

  • Image data is processed solely for the immediate task requested by the user.
  • We do not sell, rent, or commercialize your screenshots, images, or extracted text to advertisers or third-party marketers.
  • We do not utilize your personal screenshots or visual content to train public AI models.

3. Data Storage & Local Security

We prioritize a local-first architecture for your personal workspace:

  • Local-Only Storage: Your history list, saved solutions, and local app preferences are retained directly on your internal device storage.
  • No Centralized Cloud Profiling: We do not host central servers that store your identity, account details, or visual history logs.
  • Data Security: Your local application data is protected by standard Android sandbox security models.

4. Device Permissions Explained

SnapSolve AI requests specific device permissions to function correctly. You retain full control over these permissions via your Android system settings:

  • Camera Permission: Required only when taking pictures inside the App to analyze physical documents or visual problems.
  • Storage / Media Access (Visual Media URIs): Required to allow you to select existing screenshots or saved images from your photo gallery for solver processing.

5. Data Retention & User Rights (Data Deletion)

Since your solver history and image references reside entirely on your device:

  • You can clear your solver history directly within the App settings/history controls at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally cached history, databases, and configuration settings associated with com.rajesh.codecanvas.snapsolveai from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is a general utility tool and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can guide you through deleting any stored local app cache or resolving your concern.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, technical changes, or regulatory guidelines. Any modifications will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
